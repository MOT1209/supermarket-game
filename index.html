🛒 FRESH MART v2.0

Welcome to your supermarket! Stock shelves from delivery boxes, serve customers at the register, grow your store and become the biggest market in town.

How to play

• WASD to walk, Shift to sprint, mouse to look around.
• Press E to pick up a delivery box, then press E at a matching shelf to stock it.
• Empty boxes become trash — carry them to the outdoor dumpster for a recycling bonus.
• At the register press E to check out customers (card or exact cash change).
• Use the Store Terminal to order stock, buy licenses, build furniture, hire staff and rename your store.
• Your progress saves automatically. Press Esc for settings.
